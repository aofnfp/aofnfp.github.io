It's Probably Not What You Think

Here's what the research actually found: for many babies, repeated night waking isn't hunger, teething, or a "regression." It's a habit problem with a practical fix.

Somewhere between birth and now, your baby learned to fall asleep one way — nursing, rocking, being held. Now every time they surface between sleep cycles, they need that same thing to go back under. Researchers call these sleep-onset associations. You can just call it the 2 a.m. problem.

"Whatever conditions they fell asleep in are the conditions they need to fall back asleep. Change the way they fall asleep — gradually, kindly, consistently — and the wakings change with it."

That's the entire mechanism. Everything in the Reset is practice.

Why the Usual Advice Hasn't Worked

Put them down drowsy but awake — but also never let them cry. Feed on demand — but also don't feed to sleep. Never wake a sleeping baby — but also cap that nap. Every piece contradicts another, and each one arrives with total confidence.

The Reset replaces all of it with one written plan: what to fix during the day, the exact bedtime routine, and a word-for-word response script for every night waking — so you're never improvising at 3 a.m. again.
