About the imprint

Unstuck is a feeling. We publish the way out.

Unstuck Press exists for one reason: the internet is drowning in advice and starving for plans. Tips contradict each other, forums argue in circles, and the person with the actual problem is left to assemble a solution at 3 a.m. from twenty browser tabs.

So we do the opposite. For each problem we take on, we publish one coherent guide: researched, written by an author who knows the subject, reviewed by a human editor, and short enough to finish in an evening.

The quality bar

  • Researched, with sources. Claims trace back to published research, cited inside the book — not to vibes.
  • Human-reviewed. Every guide passes a second pair of human eyes for accuracy, safety, and honesty before it ships.
  • No AI slop. We don't publish raw machine output. Every book earns its page count.
  • Honest about limits. Our guides say plainly what they aren't — medical advice, magic fixes, or right for everyone.
  • Guaranteed. Every book ships with a 30-day money-back guarantee. Work the plan; if it doesn't work, you don't pay.

Faceless by design

Unstuck Press is a faceless imprint. No founder story, no personal brand, no influencer personality — because the problem is the product. A guide is only as good as whether it works, and that's the only review that matters.
